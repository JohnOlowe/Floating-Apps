## Main caption (copy from here)

She was never an afterthought.

Genesis doesn't say God made the man, realised something was missing, and improvised. It says male and female He set out to create — and He blessed *them*, and gave the mandate to *them*.

A few things from Sunday School this week 👇

→ "It is not good for man to be alone" isn't calling the man evil. Sin hadn't entered the world yet. It means alone, he was incomplete — unable by himself to carry the purpose he'd been given.

→ Adam was alone, but he was never lonely. He had God's presence and every living creature. God doesn't give you a counterpart because you feel lonely. He does it when you've been formed, placed, and are walking in His command.

→ The word for "helper" is *ezer* — the same word Scripture uses for God Himself in Psalm 121. Indispensable, life-saving strength. Not a lesser rank.

→ She was brought to him. Adam never went searching. Not from his head to rule over him, not from his foot to be trampled on — but from his side, to be his equal.

Marriage isn't defined by what the world says. It's defined by what Scripture has said.
