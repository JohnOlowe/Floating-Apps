# Caption: Concerning Spiritual Gifts (1 Corinthians 12)

## Main caption (copy from here)

The Holy Spirit is the giver of the gifts; believers who carry His indwelling are the receivers. The indwelling comes before the gifts.

Here are a few takeaways from the session👇

→ There are diversities of gifts, but one Giver, the same Spirit. They were never given for one person to be custodian. They go round, so that the Church isn't lacking in any gift.

→ Don't demean your gift, and don't exalt it. The ear doesn't stop being part of the body because it isn't the eye; and the eye can't turn to the hand and say, "I have no need of you."

→ "Helper" gifts, "platform" gifts, it doesn't matter. No believer is exempted from manifesting the gifts, and you don't need a platform to walk in them.

→ Covet them earnestly. Spiritual gifts should be the object of our desires and pursuits, all in subordination to the grace, the will and the pleasure of God.

Whatever the gift, it must be administered in love. The end of it is that God is glorified and men are edified.

## Alternate version (leads with the body imagery)

There are diversities of gifts, but one Giver, the same Spirit.

Here are a few takeaways from the session👇

→ The gifts were never handed to one person to be custodian. They go round, so that no member of the Church is left saying "this isn't for me."

→ Don't demean your gift. The ear doesn't stop being part of the body because it isn't the eye.

→ Don't exalt it either. The eye can't turn to the hand and say, "I have no need of you." There's no room for boasting in something you were given.

→ Your gift and mine are meant to complement, not compete. Same source, same purpose.

The end of it all is that God is glorified and men are edified.
