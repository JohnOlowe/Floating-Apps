## Main caption (copy from here)

God didn't make the man and then improvise the woman. Scripture says male and female He set out to create; He blessed them, and gave the mandate to them.

Here are a few takeaways from the session👇

→ "It is not good for man to be alone" isn't an insult. Sin hadn't entered the world. It means alone, he was incomplete.

→ Adam was alone, but never lonely. Loneliness isn't the signal. Being formed, placed, and walking in God's command is.

→ "Helper" = ezer. The same word used for God in Psalm 121. It means life-saving strength, not a lesser rank.

She wasn't taken from his head to rule over him, or his foot to be trampled on, but from his side, to be his equal.
